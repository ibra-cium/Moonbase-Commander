- Implemented `normalize_jobs(plan, storm_today)` (sick crew forced to `REST`, solar storm forces all crew to `REST`), `outside_flags(state)` (`REPAIR` inside when recycler broken, outside when recycler working; `REGOLITH`, `SCIENCE`, `CLEAN` outside; `FARM`, `REST` inside), `apply_daily_dose(worked_outside, eva_served)` (0.95 mSv habitat vs 1.369 mSv surface), and `apply_sickness(co2_high, jobs)` (`CO2_HEADACHE_MMHG` makes healthy crew sick for `SICK_REST_DAYS = 2` days; resting sick crew decrements `sick_days` until recovery).
- Implemented `apply_job_effects(jobs, power_served, state)` resolving end-of-day job mechanics: outside jobs with EVA shed do nothing and stay inside; `REPAIR` fixes broken recycler first (inside) and broken panels if recycler working (outside); `CLEAN` resets dust to 0; `REGOLITH` adds `REGOLITH_G_CM2_PER_JOB` to shelter; `SCIENCE` adds points if both EVA and ops power served; dust adds `DUST_PER_OUTSIDE_JOB = 0.02` per worked outside job (excluding `CLEAN`), capped at `DUST_MAX_LOSS = 0.4`.
